Privacy Policy

1.1 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you visit our website, buy or enroll in a program, work with us in coaching, or use one of our software tools. It covers three groups of people: customers and coaching clients, instructors who use our tools, and students who use a tool because their instructor assigned it.

1.2 Information we collect from customers, coaching clients, and instructors

  • Contact information: name, email address, institution, role, and similar details.
  • Account data: username, password, profile information, and communication preferences.
  • Payment and billing data: handled by third-party payment processors. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, links clicked, time on page, referring addresses, and general analytics.
  • Communications: emails, form submissions, survey responses, and coaching notes you choose to share.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and approximate location based on IP address.

1.3 Students who use our tools

Students do not buy from us and do not create an account with us. Unless a tool's own page says otherwise, a student-facing tool runs inside the student's web browser and does not send us the student's name, work, or answers.

For chalkcast, our whiteboard recording studio:

  • Students do not sign in, and we do not receive their name, video, voice, or drawing.
  • The camera and microphone are used only inside the student's browser to make the recording.
  • The recording and whiteboard are kept on the student's own device while they work, and are cleared from the browser when they finish or start over. Unfinished work that is left behind is cleared after 24 hours.
  • The student downloads the finished video and PDF and uploads them to their instructor's course. From that point the files are held by the college's course system, under the college's own policies.
  • We cannot see, recover, or delete a student's files, because we never have them.

Some tools are served from a third-party web host such as GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it may record technical data such as the visitor's IP address when a page loads.

1.4 Instructors who use our tools

Some instructor features save your entries (for example assignment prompts, rubrics, and settings) in your own browser on your own computer. Those entries are not sent to us. If a tool offers an instructor account, we store the account email and the content you choose to save in that account so it is available when you sign in. Instructor setup pages are not built to hold student names, grades, or other student records, and we ask that you not enter them.

1.5 How we use information

  • To provide, run, and improve our website, courses, coaching, resources, and tools.
  • To process payments and manage purchases, enrollments, and subscriptions.
  • To answer questions and support requests.
  • To send confirmations, renewal notices, and important updates about your account or programs.
  • To send educational content, newsletters, or marketing messages. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To understand how our offerings are used so we can improve them.
  • To find and fix security or technical problems and to meet legal obligations.

1.6 Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers that support our work, such as Kajabi (our website and course platform), payment processors, email providers, analytics tools, and the web hosts that serve our tools.
  • Professional advisors, such as attorneys or accountants, where reasonably necessary.
  • Authorities or others when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or others.
  • A successor business in a merger, acquisition, or similar transaction, subject to confidentiality protections.

1.7 Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described here, including legal, accounting, and reporting requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it where feasible.

1.8 Cookies and browser storage

Our website uses cookies. Our tools may also store settings or unfinished work in your browser on your device. See the Cookies Policy below.

1.9 Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • See the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Ask us to delete certain information, within legal and contractual limits.
  • Object to or restrict certain uses of your information.
  • Opt out of marketing messages with the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

1.10 California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, may give you additional rights, including:

  • The right to know what categories of personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • The right to access specific pieces of personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to request deletion of certain personal information.
  • The right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • The right to limit the use and disclosure of certain sensitive personal information, if collected.
  • The right to be free from discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising as California law defines those terms. If that changes, we will update this policy and provide a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" option.

To use your California privacy rights, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section. We may need to verify your identity first.

1.11 Children and student records

Our coaching, courses, and instructor tools are for adult educators and professionals. Our student-facing tools are meant for college students who use them at their instructor's direction. Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe we have, contact us so we can delete it.

Student work created with our tools becomes part of a student's education record only when the student submits it to the instructor's course. The instructor and the college are responsible for handling those records under FERPA and their own policies.

1.12 Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows the most recent revision.

Cookies Policy

6.1 What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site work, remember your preferences, and show how visitors use the content.

6.2 How we use cookies

  • Essential cookies: needed for basic functions such as secure login and page navigation.
  • Preference cookies: remember settings such as language or display choices.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
  • Marketing or performance cookies: in limited cases, track engagement with emails or campaigns.

6.3 Browser storage in our tools

Our tools may save settings (such as a light or dark view) or unfinished work in your browser's storage on your own device so you do not lose your place. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it.

6.4 Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be placed by third-party services we use, such as Kajabi, payment processors, analytics providers, or embedded content hosted on other platforms. These third parties may collect information about your online activity over time and across websites.

6.5 Managing cookies

You can control or turn off cookies in your browser settings. Turning off some cookies may affect how our site works.

Accessibility Statement

7.1 Our commitment

MedMasters Collaborative, LLC is committed to making our website, learning materials, and tools accessible to educators and students, including people with disabilities. Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA.

7.2 Ongoing efforts

  • Clear headings, structured content, and readable text.
  • Strong color contrast and legible fonts.
  • Keyboard access, visible focus, and labels that work with screen readers.
  • Captions or transcripts for audio and video content where feasible. For student recordings made with our tools, captions are added by the instructor's course system when that feature is available.
  • Written accessibility notes for our tools, available on request.

7.3 We welcome feedback

If you run into a barrier or have a specific accessibility need, please tell us. Students who need an accommodation for an assignment should contact their instructor first.
