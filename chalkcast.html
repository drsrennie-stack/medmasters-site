chalkcast · Whiteboard recording studio

Watch your students teach it back.

chalkcast is a whiteboard recording studio for graded teaching assignments. Students draw, talk through their reasoning on camera, and turn in a video you can grade.

What your students do

One link. Nothing to install.

01
Click the link in the assignment.
There is nothing to install and no account to make.
02
Turn on the camera and microphone.
Both are checked before the whiteboard opens.
03
Solve your problem on the whiteboard.
They draw and explain it out loud while chalkcast records the board, the camera, and their voice together.
04
Download and upload.
They download a video and a PDF of their whiteboard, and upload both to your course.

Who it is for

Instructors who ask students to explain how they got an answer, in courses like anatomy, physiology, nursing, chemistry, and math.

What you need
  • Students use a computer with Chrome, Edge, or Safari, plus a camera and a microphone.
  • You need a course site that accepts file uploads. Canvas Studio is optional and adds automatic captions.

Questions

What instructors ask.

Do my students need accounts?
+
No. They click your link.
Where do the videos go?
+
Only where the student uploads them, which is your own course site. chalkcast never receives them.
Does it work on a tablet?
+
chalkcast is built for mouse, touch, and stylus. It has been tested on desktop Chrome. Tablet testing is in progress, so for now we recommend a computer.
Are the videos captioned?
+
chalkcast does not make captions itself. If your college has Canvas Studio, the instructions send the video through it, and Studio adds automatic captions for you to review.
Can I try it first?
+
Yes. The demo gives you a sample problem and a one-minute recording.
